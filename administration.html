The administration

The Government of the Irad Special Economic Zone is led by the Governor and the Lieutenant Governor. The Lieutenant Governor chairs the cabinet of ministers and heads of departments. Ministries and departments are listed below with their heads where appointed.

Office of the Governor

Governor

Erlan Toktarov

Head of the executive. On extended health retreat since July 2024.

Lieutenant Governor

Aidar Maksat

Chairs the cabinet and the ISEZ Security Council; conducts the day-to-day business of government.

Investment Promotion Office

Kirill Zhdanov

Single counter for foreign investors; keeps the register of investment agreements.

Public Integrity Commission

Established 2014

Investigates misconduct by public officials and agrees settlements.

External Relations Office

Office of the Lieutenant Governor

Trade offices abroad; liaison with UNMI and the bodies of the 2007 ceasefire and the Naryn Protocol.

Rural Development Fund

Office of the Lieutenant Governor

Co-finances small enterprises in the districts: workshops, small hydro stations, processing lines.

Ministries and departments

Ministry of Internal Security

Deputy minister for the southern districts: Jenishbek Tursunaliev

Police detachments in the six districts, the Republican Gendarmerie, passports and residence registration, the Corrections Directorate.

Ministry of Natural Resources

Minister: Chingiz Asanov

Mining concessions and ore licensing, water plans, rangers and protected areas, crop production.

Ministry of Economy

 

Economic policy, the register of firms, trade licensing; the Talas Basin Agricultural Directorate.

Ministry of Health

 

Hospitals and polyclinics with the city governments; emergency treatment is free.

Department of Public Information

 

Government press office; registration of publishers and printers; performance and screening permits.

Department of Infrastructure and Communications

 

Roads, telecommunications regulation and the e-Irad public services portal.

Department of Energy

 

Energy policy; owner of Iradenergo.

Department of Education

Curriculum section: Dinara Seitkulova

Public schools, the school building programme and textbooks.

Heritage Department

 

Protected monuments and open-air museums, including Rabat-i-Safed; excavation permits.

Agencies and services

Republican Gendarmerie

Commander: Maj. Gen. Ruslan Toktarov

The Qara-Chik line checkpoints, the Border Demarcation Brigade, the Highway Patrol Corps and the Special Protective Service.

State Defense Corps

 

The armed forces of the ISEZ, formed in July 2007.

Customs Service

Deputy director: Anvar Sultanbekov

Customs posts at the frontier crossings; the Talas Customs Clearance Terminal.

Territorial Statistics Office

Director: Gulbarchyn Ryskulova

Official statistics; the 2018 census and the 2026 census of business entities.

Irad Foreign Trade Escrow Authority

 

Supervises export escrow accounts and deducts royalties (1994).

Talas River Hydrological Monitoring Service

 

River gauges, the glacier inventory and the basin forecasts for each irrigation season.

National Equestrian Sports Directorate

 

Traditional horse sports, the Irad State Hippodrome and the pari-mutuel tote.

State Archives of Irad

 

Archive fonds since the Kokand charters; legal deposit numbers for publications.

Attorney General

 

Criminal prosecutions and applications to the courts on behalf of the Government.

Independent institutions

Bank of Irad

Chairman: Azamat Temirov · Deputy chairman: Ermek Sadykbekov

Issues the som under currency-board rules, at 8.40 to the US dollar since 17 March 2008; supervises banks.

Territorial Legislature (Mejlis)

Speaker: Nurbek Sultanov

60 members elected for four years in eight constituencies. Legislative Hall, Talas.

Supreme Court of Irad

Five justices

Hall of Justice, Liberty Boulevard, Talas. Register of pending cases published online since October 2026.

Territorial Election Commission

Seven members

Voter registers, registration of candidates and results. Next general election: 2 November 2027.

State enterprises

Iradenergo

State joint-stock company (2008): Toktogul and the Naryn cascade, the high-voltage grid, the National Dispatch Centre; Talas and Nurzhan Electric Networks.

Irad Pochtasy

State postal enterprise (2008): about 190 post offices, pensions and allowances at the counter, money orders. Head office: Talas Central Post Office.

Iradstrakh

State Insurance Company of Irad: compulsory motor third-party policies, property, crop and livestock insurance; about 60 offices.

Irad Temir Jolu

Railways: about 690 km of line from the Kazakh frontier through Nurzhan and Talas.

IradOne

State public broadcaster: IradOne, IradOdin and the Radio Irad network.

Ministries and departments can also be reached through their id.gov addresses. For services to the public, use e-Irad.
